FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have another question? Send us a message.

What are common signs an AC system needs repair?

Warm air from the vents, weak airflow, unusual noises, water around the indoor unit, ice on refrigerant lines, frequent cycling or a system that won’t turn on are all signs that a technician should take a look.

How often should HVAC systems be serviced?

A professional check at least once a year is a common recommendation. Systems that both heat and cool — such as heat pumps — are often serviced twice a year, before cooling season and before heating season.

What factors affect HVAC installation costs?

System type and size, efficiency level, the condition of existing ductwork, electrical and refrigerant line requirements, venting for gas equipment, accessibility and added accessories such as thermostats or air quality equipment all influence cost.

What is the difference between a heat pump and a furnace?

A furnace creates heat by burning fuel or using electric resistance. A heat pump moves heat using refrigerant — pulling heat into the home in winter and removing it in summer — so it provides both heating and cooling.

Why might an air conditioner struggle to cool a home?

Common causes include dirty filters, blocked returns, dirty coils, low refrigerant from a leak, failed fan motors or capacitors, leaky ductwork or equipment that isn’t properly sized for the home.

When should a homeowner consider replacing an aging system?

Replacement is worth considering when a system is older and needs frequent or costly repairs, when comfort problems persist, when a major component such as a compressor or heat exchanger fails, or when efficiency has dropped noticeably.
