Why Is My AC Running but Not Cooling?

Warm air from the vents has several possible causes. Here’s what you can safely check — and what needs a professional.

6 min readAervanta Heating & Cooling

Few things are more frustrating on a hot Charlotte afternoon than hearing the air conditioner running while the house keeps getting warmer. The good news is that the cause often falls into a handful of categories — and a few are simple to check.

Start with the simple checks

  • Thermostat settings: Make sure it’s set to “cool,” the setpoint is below room temperature and the fan is on “auto.” A fan set to “on” keeps blowing air even when the system isn’t cooling.
  • Air filter: A clogged filter restricts airflow and can cause the indoor coil to freeze. Replace it if it looks dirty.
  • Vents and returns: Furniture, rugs or curtains blocking registers reduce airflow.
  • Outdoor unit: Check that it’s running and that leaves, grass clippings or overgrown plants aren’t blocking it.
  • Breakers: A tripped breaker can shut down the outdoor unit while the indoor fan keeps running. If it trips again after resetting, leave it off and call for service.

Common causes that need a technician

Frozen evaporator coil

Restricted airflow or low refrigerant can cause ice to form on the indoor coil. Turn the system off and set the fan to “on” to help it thaw, then have it inspected.

Low refrigerant

Air conditioners don’t use up refrigerant. If the charge is low, there’s a leak. Finding and repairing it requires certified technicians and specialized equipment.

Failed capacitor or fan motor

If the outdoor fan isn’t spinning or the unit hums without starting, an electrical component may have failed. These parts store electrical energy and should only be handled by a professional.

Dirty condenser coil

Dirt and pollen on the outdoor coil make it harder for the system to release heat, reducing cooling capacity on the hottest days.

Ductwork problems

Disconnected or leaky ducts in an attic or crawlspace can dump cooled air where you don’t need it.

When to call

If the simple checks don’t solve the problem, or you notice ice, water leaks, burning smells or a tripping breaker, turn the system off and schedule service. Learn more about AC repair.

This article provides general information. Electrical, gas and refrigerant work should only be performed by trained, qualified HVAC professionals.
