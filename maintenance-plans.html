Why It Matters

Why Routine Maintenance Matters

HVAC systems work hardest during the hottest and coldest weeks of the year. Routine maintenance gives a technician the chance to find worn parts, dirty coils and clogged drains before they cause a breakdown.

  • Helps equipment operate closer to its designed performance
  • Finds small issues before they become larger repairs
  • Keeps safety controls and drainage checked
  • Supports manufacturer maintenance recommendations
What’s Reviewed

What a Maintenance Visit Covers

Air conditioning inspection

Coils, refrigerant circuit indicators, electrical components, outdoor fan and temperature split.

Heating system inspection

Burners, ignition, safety controls and venting on furnaces; defrost and auxiliary heat on heat pumps.

Air filter checks

Filter condition, fit and type, matched to your equipment’s airflow.

Condensate drain inspection

Drain lines, pans and safety switches that prevent overflow and water damage.

Equipment operation assessment

Start-up, cycling, airflow and thermostat response under normal operation.

Seasonal service planning

Cooling checks in spring and heating checks in fall, timed before peak demand.
